I am a **PhD candidate** at the **University of Melbourne**, interested in **computer graphics** and **deep learning**. My research centers on novel-view synthesis and immersive experiences — including **3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)**, **Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF)**, and **mixed / augmented reality**. Outside research I enjoy music, exercise, and learning anything new. Please feel free to reach out if you share similar interests!

Research interests
======
- Computer graphics and novel-view synthesis (3D Gaussian Splatting, NeRF)
- Sampling and perceptual quality in radiance-field rendering
- Mixed reality, augmented reality, and immersive XR experiences
- Visual effects (VFX)
- Deep learning for graphics
